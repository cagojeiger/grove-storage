src/template contains adaptations of the MUI Dashboard, Sign-in, and
shared-theme templates, including community Data Grid and chart styles.

Source revision: 27565bf06476f6ce2f8eb0c67393c67477968fa2
https://github.com/mui/material-ui/tree/27565bf06476f6ce2f8eb0c67393c67477968fa2/docs/data/material/getting-started/templates

Grove adaptations: system font, green brand scale, neutral sign-in background,
product navigation and permissions, CSP nonce, accessible menu identifiers,
external field labels, multiline forms, and severity-specific MUI alerts.
The shared theme applies to the entire console. Existing API and session
contracts are preserved. Demo data, workspace selection, advertising, search,
social sign-in, registration, and unsupported recovery controls are excluded.
